About World at War Environment Previewer

Preview lighting, skies, Vision and fog in the included scene. The Direct3D 9 viewport shows the placed models and authored FX.

Bake to update lighting and sky changes. Vision, fog and visibility controls update immediately. Requires your own installed WaW/Modtools assets. No gameplay or map scripts are executed.

Camera & framing

Match your in-game view while resizing this app freely. The fitted frame keeps the game aspect ratio and renders at the chosen resolution.

Change width and height when you change WaW's video mode. Matching FOV and framing also needs a matching camera position and direction; ADS, sprint and scripted camera changes are not simulated.
