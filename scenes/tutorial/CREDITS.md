# Sniperbolt’s Zombies tutorial

Sniperbolt created the layout in *Nazi Zombie Tutorial v2.0* (3 February 2010). The original tutorial credits Sparks for its scripts. The bundled `scene.map` is the unchanged `map_source/nazi_zombie_tutorial.map` from that distribution. Its SHA-256 is `c6339714b33874a0af695532322c75a70d3fd0c3b60209dfbde24a0b3a86da46`.

The FX entry in `profile.json` reproduces the initial mystery-box marker. The tutorial enables the box light, selects the authored `start_chest`, follows its lid and anchor targets, and applies a −90° pitch offset. The installed `maps/zombie/fx_zombie_factory_marker` effect supplies the visuals. The original CreateFX scripts have empty entry points. The camera, fog presets and other preview defaults are configured in the same profile.

The original tutorial readme permits editing the map. It does not specify a redistribution licence. The application's GPL licence covers the previewer code. World at War and Modtools provide the stock assets and compilers.
